🎮 About the Game
Mythical Plantation is a magical farming simulation game where you cultivate rare and mythical plants, each with their own unique properties, personalities, and stories. Step into the shoes of a budding botanist-magician and restore the legendary Garden of Aethel to its former glory!

✨ Key Features
🌱 Cultivate Mythical Flora - Grow plants from ancient myths and legends

🔮 Alchemical Crafting - Brew potions and create magical items

🏡 Farm Customization - Design and expand your enchanted garden

📖 Lore-Rich World - Uncover the secrets of each magical species

🤝 Character Relationships - Befriend mystical creatures and fellow gardeners

🌟 Seasonal Events - Participate in magical festivals and competitions

## 🎮 About the Game

**Mythical Plantation** is a magical farming simulation game where you cultivate rare and mythical plants, each with their own unique properties, personalities, and stories. Step into the shoes of a budding botanist-magician and restore the legendary Garden of Aethel to its former glory!

### ✨ Key Features

- 🌱 **Cultivate Mythical Flora** - Grow plants from ancient myths and legends
- 🔮 **Alchemical Crafting** - Brew potions and create magical items
- 🏡 **Farm Customization** - Design and expand your enchanted garden
- 📖 **Lore-Rich World** - Uncover the secrets of each magical species
- 🤝 **Character Relationships** - Befriend mystical creatures and fellow gardeners
- 🌟 **Seasonal Events** - Participate in magical festivals and competitions
